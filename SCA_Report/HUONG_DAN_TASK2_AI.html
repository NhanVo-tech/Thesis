6. Intent co nen dung de ra quyet dinh control khong? (tra loi)

Cau tra loi: CO, nen dung - nhung AI chi GATE (cho phep/chan) quyet dinh, khong tu dong quyet dinh.

  • Logic hien tai (AccessController) da dung toan hoc: vi tri + van toc + vung (zone) de mo/khoa cua. No an toan va de giai thich, nhung co the bi danh lua boi truong hop di ngang qua cham (pass-by) ma giam toc nhe.
  • AI doan y dinh (approach / seated / leave / passing) giup phan biet "thuc su muon vao" voi "di ngang qua" ma logic toan hoc kho phan biet.
  • Nguyen tac (theo docs/AI_INTENT_RECOGNITION.md): AI khong truc tiep bam relay. AI chi nang/hang rao tin cay cho cac chuyen doi cua AccessController. Neu AI loi (thieu model, het RAM) thi he thong van chay duoc bang logic toan hoc (fail-safe).
IntentQuyet dinh ma master nen dua ra
approach (p >= 0.80)Cho phep mo cua (khi da o vung DRIVER_DOOR + dung lai)
seated (p >= 0.80)Khoa cua + cho phep no may (khi o ghe lai)
leave (p >= 0.80)Khoa cua khi ra khoi xe
passing (p >= 0.70)Chan cung khoa - khong mo du gi ca (chong unlock sai)
Da wire xong (da build OK): AccessController::handlePosition(x, y, vx, vy, intent) nhan them mang 4 xac suat (hoac nullptr khi model chua san sang). Gate: unlock can p_approach >= 0.80; ngoi can p_seated >= 0.80; roi di can p_leave >= 0.80; p_passing >= 0.70 chan cung khoa. Neu intent == nullptr (warm-up / thieu model) thi bo qua gate - fail-safe.
