Task 1 - Phan tich EKF (Extended Kalman Filter)

Muc tieu: chung minh bang so lieu va hinh anh rang bo loc EKF giup lam muot (giam nhieu) vi tri tinh tu trilateration nhu the nao, de dua vao bao cao / luan van.

Cong cu: iot\tools\analyze_ekf.py - doc log serial cua ESP32, parse cac dong [POS2D] (ket qua trilateration tho) va [EKF] (vi tri da loc), roi in bang so lieu + ve bieu do.

1. Loi da gap va cach khac phuc (da xu ly)

Loi ban dau:

FileNotFoundError: [Errno 2] No such file or directory:
'C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\ekf_noise.png'

Nguyen nhan: thu muc C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\ chua ton tai, trong khi script khong tu tao thu muc cha truoc khi luu anh.

Da sua xong: da bo sung vao analyze_ekf.py (ham _finish) lenh tu tao thu muc bang os.makedirs(..., exist_ok=True). Tu gio khong can tu tao thu muc nua.

Luu y quan trong ve ket qua lan chay dau tien: voi capture.log co san, ket qua la noise reduction = 1.10x (rat thap). Day khong phai loi - vi capture.log la du lieu nguoi dang di bo, khong phai tag dung yen. Chi khi tag dung yen, bo loc EKF moi the hien ro tac dung (std giam manh). Xem Buoc 3 de thu du lieu dung yen.

2. Cac mode cua analyze_ekf.py

ModeKet qua
metricsBang so lieu console: mean / std / max deviation / noise-reduction ratio
noiseBieu do chuoi thoi gian 1 truc toa do: raw vs EKF (dung cho tag dung yen)
trajectoryBieu do 2D: cac diem trilateration tho vs duong di EKF (kem vong tron unlock 2m)
sweepLoc lai du lieu tho voi nhieu gia tri kAccelStd roi chong len nhau (de chon tham so)
allmetrics + noise + trajectory (mac dinh)

Cu phap chung:

python analyze_ekf.py --log capture.log [--mode MODE] [--axis x|y] [--out PREFIX] [--sweep-kaccel ...]

3. Cac buoc thuc hien chi tiet

1Chay thu tren log co san (khong can phan cung)

Dung de xac nhan tool chay on. Log C:\DATN\capture.log da co san (81 mau POS2D, 178 mau EKF).

& "C:\DATN\venv\Scripts\python.exe" "C:\DATN\SmartCarAccess\iot\tools\analyze_ekf.py" --log "C:\DATN\capture.log" --mode all --out "C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\ekf"

Ket qua: bang metrics + 2 file ekf_noise.png va ekf_trajectory.png trong C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\.

2Thu log tag DUNG YEN (so lieu quan trong nhat)

Day la so lieu the hien ro tac dung giam nhieu cua EKF: tag dung yen -> raw jitter khoang 10-30 cm, EKF gan nhu nam ngang (std < 5 cm).

Thiet lap truoc khi chay (chi can 1 lan cho moi cua so PowerShell):

$env:PYTHONPATH = "C:\DATN\DW3_QM33_SDK_1.1.1\SDK\Tools\uwb-qorvo-tools\lib\uwb-uci;C:\DATN\DW3_QM33_SDK_1.1.1\SDK\Tools\uwb-qorvo-tools\lib\uqt-utils"

Chay demo va capture ra file ekf_static.log (dung --autostart de anchor chay ngay; dat tag DUNG YEN canh cua 20-30 giay roi Ctrl+C):

& "C:\DATN\venv\Scripts\python.exe" "C:\DATN\SmartCarAccess\iot\tools\localization_demo.py" -p COM11 COM19 COM12 --macs 0 1 2 --esp-port COM5 --autostart --capture ekf_static.log

Sau do phan tich (mode noise, truc X):

& "C:\DATN\venv\Scripts\python.exe" "C:\DATN\SmartCarAccess\iot\tools\analyze_ekf.py" --log ekf_static.log --axis x --mode noise --out "C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\ekf_static"

Lap lai voi --axis y neu muon ca hai truc.

3Thu log DI BO (cho bieu do quy dao)

Di vong quanh xe (hoac di toi cua roi dung) khoang 30 giay, capture tuong tu:

Phan tich quy dao 2D:

4Sweep kAccelStd (chon tham so co can cu)

Chay tren log dung yen, thu nhieu gia tri kAccelStd de giai thich vi sao chon gia tri hien tai (1.2 trong firmware):

& "C:\DATN\venv\Scripts\python.exe" "C:\DATN\SmartCarAccess\iot\tools\analyze_ekf.py" --log ekf_static.log --axis x --mode sweep --sweep-kaccel 0.3,0.6,1.0,1.5,2.5 --out "C:\DATN\SCA_Report\fig\ekf_sweep"

Chon gia tri kAccelStd nho nhat ma duong loc van bam duoc quy dao that (can bang giua lag khi re va nhieu lot qua).

4. Cach doc ket qua (bang metrics)

Chi soY nghia
meanDo lech tam (bias) cua uoc luong quanh tam cua no
std (stationary)Do phan tan: raw nen ~10-30 cm, filtered < ~5 cm
max |dev|Do lech toi nhat cua 1 mau (bien do nhieu dinh)
noise reductionraw_std / filtered_std; > 3x la ket qua lam muot tot

Luu y: metrics tinh tren TOAN BO log. De so lieu "dung yen" co y nghia, phai capture mot doan tag DUNG YEN (hoac cat log chi giu doan dung yen).

5. Ket qua mong doi (de dua vao bao cao)

  • Tag dung yen: noise reduction dat khoang 3x - 6x (vi du: raw std 12 cm, filtered std 2-3 cm).
  • Bieu do noise: duong xanh (raw) giao dong manh, duong do (EKF) gan nhu thang.
  • Bieu do trajectory: duong do (EKF) ban theo cum diem xanh (raw) ma khong bi giat lung tung.
  • Bieu do sweep: giai thich vi sao kAccelStd = 1.2 la lua chon can bang.
